If you are staying in one of our hospitals, you will be provided with breakfast, lunch and an evening meal. If you arrive in between meal times you should be offered a snack box, however please don’t hesitate to ask ward staff for one. We are able to cater for any special dietary requirements, allergies and religious requirements. If possible, please let us know of these requirements in advance or speak to a member of staff when you arrive. Most of our hospitals have either a restaurant or a café where you or your visitors can get hot and cold meals, snacks and refreshments. A snack trolley also comes round most wards during the day. Please ask ward staff for more information.
