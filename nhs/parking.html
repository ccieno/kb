Here is the information for patient and visitor car parking at the Royal Casterbridge Hospital.

We have several car parks for patients and visitors. All use an Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) system. This means a camera records your number plate on entry. You must pay at a pay station before you return to your car.

Parking Locations

  • Car Park A (Main Entrance): This is our largest car park, located directly opposite the main hospital entrance. It is for patients and visitors.

  • Car Park C (Outpatients): This car park is for the Outpatients Centre, Dermatology, and the Eye Clinic.

  • Car Park D (Emergency Dept / A&E): This car park has a 20-minute drop-off/pick-up zone. It is intended for A&E patients and visitors only.

Parking Charges

Parking charges apply 24/7. You can pay by card or cash at the pay stations located at the exits of Car Parks A and C.

  • Up to 30 minutes: Free (for pick-up and drop-off)

  • Up to 1 hour: £2.50

  • Up to 2 hours: £4.00

  • Up to 4 hours: £6.00

  • Up to 6 hours: £8.00

  • 6 to 24 hours: £10.00

  • 7-day ticket: £25.00 (This can be purchased from the main reception desk)

Free Parking & Concessions

We offer free parking for certain patient and visitor groups. You must register your vehicle to receive free parking.

Who is eligible for free parking?

  • Blue Badge Holders: All Blue Badge holders can park for free in designated disabled bays or any standard bay.

  • Frequent Outpatient Attenders: Patients who attend hospital for an appointment at least 3 times within a month, for at least 3 consecutive months.

  • Parents of Sick Children: Parents or guardians of a child who is an inpatient and staying overnight (one vehicle per family)

  • Patients attending for Cancer or Renal Treatment: Free parking is provided for patients attending for chemotherapy, radiotherapy, or renal dialysis.

  • End-of-Life Care: The next of kin for patients receiving end-of-life care.

How to claim your concession:

  • Blue Badge Holders: On your first visit, please take your Blue Badge and vehicle registration to the Main Reception desk. We will register your car on our system for the duration of your badge's validity.

  • All Other Concessions: Please speak to the staff on the ward or in the department you are visiting. They will provide you with a form or validate your parking internally.
